## 🎭 Main Characters

* **Morton Schmidt** (*Jonah Hill*)
  * The smart, socially awkward cop who surprisingly becomes popular when going back to high school.
* **Greg Jenko** (*Channing Tatum*)
  * The athletic, handsome cop who isn't very bright and gets placed with the science geeks.
* **Captain Dickson** (*Ice Cube*)
  * The short-tempered police captain in charge of the undercover Jump Street program.

## 🍿 Synopsis & Plot Highlights

Two high school enemies-turned-rookie police officers end up in a secret undercover unit. Their mission is to infiltrate a local high school to stop the spread of a dangerous synthetic drug called **HFS** (*Holy F***ing Shit*).

### Major Plot Steps:
1. **Academy Days:** Schmidt and Jenko bond at the police academy.
2. **The Assignment:** After a botched bust, they get reassigned to *21 Jump Street*.
3. **Role Reversal:** Schmidt becomes the cool guy, while Jenko hangs out with the tech crowd.
4. **The Investigation:** They track down the drug supplier while preparing for the school play and prom.
